## Vulnerabilities

### [Critical OS Command Injection Vulnerabilities in 3DEXPERIENCE Station Launcher](https://threatcluster.io/cluster/critical-os-command-injection-vulnerabilities-in-3dexperienc-f61b94a6)

On November 16, 2026, Dassault Systèmes disclosed two critical OS command injection vulnerabilities in the Station Launcher App of the 3DEXPERIENCE platform, identified as CVE-2026-19732 and CVE-2026-77033. These vulnerabilities affect releases R2023x through R2026x, allowing low-privileged authenticated users to execute arbitrary commands on the server, potentially compromising the entire platform. The vulnerabilities have been rated with a CVSS score of 9.9, indicating a critical severity level. Patches have been released, and users are advised to update to the latest versions to mitigate the risks. There is currently no evidence of public exploitation or proof-of-concept code. The vulnerabilities were detailed in multiple advisories, emphasizing the urgency of patching affected systems. Organizations using the 3DEXPERIENCE platform should prioritize remediation to protect their environments.

### [Critical PHP Vulnerabilities Exploited by AI-Driven Attacks](https://threatcluster.io/cluster/critical-php-vulnerabilities-exploited-by-ai-driven-attacks-c9fd0e45)

Recent vulnerabilities in the PHP programming language have been discovered, allowing attackers to bypass login processes and execute malicious code remotely. These flaws, affecting software used by three-quarters of the world's websites, had been known for years but were only recently exploited due to advancements in AI. In 2026, nearly 900 vulnerabilities have been confirmed as exploited, nearly double the total from 2021. The rapid pace of AI-assisted vulnerability discovery has put pressure on organizations to address these issues proactively. Research indicates that over 20,000 zero-day threats were identified, with a significant portion rated as high or critical severity. The vulnerabilities highlight the need for a comprehensive threat management strategy that incorporates AI tools to mitigate risks effectively.

*2 sources · score 68 · Nova*

### [Investment Scams Target Seniors, Causing $27.5 Million in Losses](https://threatcluster.io/cluster/investment-scams-target-seniors-causing-275-million-in-losse-03922835)

Investment scams have drained $27.5 million from older Americans in the first eight months of 2026, according to a study by Homewatch CareGivers using data from the Better Business Bureau. This type of fraud has the highest financial impact among scams targeting seniors, with Las Vegas noted as the city with the highest rate of fraud. The study analyzed over 130,000 reported scams, revealing that investment fraud, despite being lower in volume, results in significant losses. Online purchase fraud follows closely, costing victims $25 million. The data highlights the vulnerability of seniors, particularly in states like California and Texas, where investment fraud losses were substantial. The findings emphasize the need for financial advisors to be vigilant against elder fraud, especially as scams become more sophisticated. The report also notes that loneliness and financial precarity are strong predictors of victimization among older adults.

## Ransomware

### [Kazakh National Extradited for $500M Ransomware Campaign Targeting U.S. Healthcare](https://threatcluster.io/cluster/kazakh-national-extradited-for-500m-ransomware-campaign-targ-ee068c18)

Roman Khlynovskiy, a 43-year-old Kazak national, was extradited from Italy to the U.S. to face charges related to his involvement in the '8-Digits Team' ransomware group. This group allegedly executed a major extortion campaign against U.S. hospitals, healthcare entities, and schools, resulting in over $500 million in ransom demands. The indictment claims that the conspirators targeted victims in at least six states, stealing sensitive patient information and threatening to release it unless ransoms were paid. The FBI emphasized the seriousness of these crimes, which exploited the personal medical data of millions. Khlynovskiy is charged with conspiracy to commit computer fraud and identity theft, among other offenses. The U.S. Attorney's Office is committed to pursuing cybercriminals regardless of their location. The case highlights ongoing efforts to combat international cybercrime and protect vulnerable populations from exploitation.

## Breaches

### [Trezor Data Breach Exposes 27,000 Customer Orders](https://threatcluster.io/cluster/trezor-data-breach-exposes-27000-customer-orders-9dfd2c53)

Trezor has confirmed a data breach at its shipping partner Skladon, exposing personal information linked to approximately 27,000 orders. The breach occurred on November 7, 2026, and was disclosed on November 16, 2026. Affected data includes names, addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, and purchase details for orders placed between August 9 and November 7. Trezor stated that its own systems remain secure and that no wallet backups were compromised. Customers have reported receiving scam calls related to the exposed information. Trezor is implementing an Anonymous Pickup service to enhance customer privacy and is reviewing its data retention policies. The company has notified relevant data protection authorities and is investigating the breach further.

*2 sources · score 56*

### [Caribbean Airlines Data Breach Exposes Passenger Information via Amadeus](https://threatcluster.io/cluster/caribbean-airlines-data-breach-exposes-passenger-information-a1b5bfde)

Caribbean Airlines has confirmed a data breach affecting passenger booking and travel information, linked to its reservations service provider, Amadeus IT Group. The breach, which occurred on November 5, 2026, allowed unauthorized access to booking records, impacting several international airlines using the same provider. Preliminary findings indicate that no payment card information was compromised, but personal details such as names and travel itineraries may have been exposed. The airline is actively notifying affected customers and has stated that Amadeus has addressed the security vulnerability. The breach has raised concerns regarding data protection compliance across jurisdictions. An investigation is ongoing, and Caribbean Airlines has apologized for any inconvenience caused.

### [Corma Secures $125 Million Amid Surge in Cybersecurity Threats](https://threatcluster.io/cluster/corma-secures-125-million-amid-surge-in-cybersecurity-threat-ff1eab5c)

Corma, an AI cybersecurity startup, raised $125 million in a Series A funding round, achieving a $1 billion valuation just three months after its launch. The funding follows a series of critical vulnerabilities disclosed in October, termed 'Black October', which affected widely used software like PHP and Node.js. These incidents prompted significant demand for Corma's AI-driven cybersecurity solutions, which integrate with existing security teams to enhance defense capabilities. The company reported that many enterprises had already deployed its technology during the crisis, which helped mitigate the impact of the vulnerabilities. Corma's models are designed to adapt dynamically to emerging threats, reflecting a shift in cybersecurity paradigms. The startup aims to expand its compute capacity to meet the rising demand for its services.

## Also worth knowing

### [AI Threatens Critical Infrastructure: Bill Gates Warns of Cyber Risks](https://threatcluster.io/cluster/ai-threatens-critical-infrastructure-bill-gates-warns-of-cyb-22dcbcb8)

Bill Gates has warned that advanced AI systems can assist cybercriminals in identifying vulnerabilities in critical infrastructure, including hospitals, power grids, and banks. He highlighted that these AI models could exploit weaknesses in interconnected software systems that support essential services. Gates expressed concern over open-weight models, which can have safety restrictions removed, making their misuse harder to detect. He predicted a significant increase in cyberattacks as malicious actors gain access to more capable AI tools. The warning emphasizes the urgent need for improved cybersecurity measures across critical sectors. Gates noted that replacing vulnerable software could take up to five years, even with adequate resources. The discussion reflects a growing recognition of AI's dual role as both a tool for innovation and a potential weapon for cybercrime.
