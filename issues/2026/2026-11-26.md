## Ransomware

### [Median ransom payment rises 167% as attackers shift to data-theft extortion](https://threatcluster.io/cluster/median-ransom-payment-rises-167-as-attackers-shift-to-data-t-a7c4a224)

A new report reports that cybercriminals increasingly threaten to expose stolen data rather than encrypt systems. According to the Ynetnews article, nearly 80% of data-theft-only cases end without payment, and experts warn that paying offers little protection against further extortion. The Ynetnews article reports that the median ransom payment rose 167%. Veeam's Cyber Secure marketing page states that cyber extortion cases frequently involve lateral movement and data exfiltration, and that ransomware attacks have targeted backup repositories, citing Coveware by Veeam Q2 2026 and Veeam 2025 Ransomware Trends reports. Ynetnews recommends the 3-2-1-1-0 backup rule, isolation of backup infrastructure from identity systems, malware scanning of backups before restoration, and quarterly recovery drills. The Veeam article is a product page promoting paid incident response, retainer and advisory services, and its statistics are presented as vendor-sourced. No specific CVEs, threat actors or incident victims are named in either article. The articles do not describe a single discrete incident; the status of the trend is ongoing.

### [N0N ransomware group claims exposure of pediatric oncology firm Day One Biopharmaceuticals data](https://threatcluster.io/cluster/n0n-ransomware-group-claims-exposure-of-pediatric-oncology-f-6cb26bea)

The N0N ransomware group listed Day One Biopharmaceuticals, Inc., a U.S. pediatric oncology company, on its leak site with the status 'NOT PAID — EXPOSED', according to Ransomware.live and RedPacket Security. The post is dated November 18, 2026, which is the listing date, not a confirmed intrusion date. The group claims 31,710,052 rows (8.6 GB) of electronic medical record extracts covering more than 143,000 patients. It also claims over 144,000 patient treatment-status records for hundreds of cancer patients, including diagnoses, genetic mutation status, therapies, pharmacy and payer information, and prescriber identities. Additional claimed material includes a directory of more than 16,000 prescribers with NPI numbers and over 750,000 field-engagement and marketing records. RedPacket Security states that listings attributed to N0N have included unverified or fabricated victim claims and that this post should be treated as unconfirmed. Ransomware.live states that listings reflect criminal claims it has not verified. The post does not state a ransom amount, does not confirm encryption, and includes no screenshots. ## Breaches

### [Bithumb Breach Exposes Passport Numbers and Wallet Addresses; Intrusion Lasted 15 Days](https://threatcluster.io/cluster/bithumb-breach-exposes-passport-numbers-and-wallet-addresses-e9f53544)

South Korean crypto exchange Bithumb said on November 25, 2026 that its October hack of the internal Daou Office mobile groupware system exposed more people and more data than first disclosed. Bithumb now lists 19 data types, including passport numbers, driver's license numbers, bank account numbers, transaction histories and wallet addresses. Its revised timeline states an unauthorised party accessed the system from 1:49 p.m. on October 12 to 3:22 p.m. on October 27 (Korea time), rather than the one hour it first reported. Bithumb originally said data on 6,444 customers and 715 employees (7,159 people) was exposed; it has not given an updated count, and Biz.Chosun reports the additional affected population is undisclosed. Bithumb says the attack was not a direct intrusion into its exchange services or customer database, and no cryptocurrency was stolen according to MLQ.ai. The exchange blocked the attacker's IP addresses, fixed the vulnerability, began a security review of externally exposed services, and increased monitoring of unusual trades and withdrawals. It warned users about phishing impersonating Bithumb and said affected users will be notified individually by email and text. Bithumb has promised compensation for confirmed harm and set up a victim support desk. Coverage rests on the company's own updated notice, with secondary reporting from Bitcoin Magazine, MLQ.ai (citing CoinDesk), Biz.Chosun and Yonhap.
